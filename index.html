How it works

Seven stages. The loop actually closes.

Most stacks stop at meeting booked. This one analyses the meeting and uses what it learned to rewrite the next campaign.

1

Source

Build the target list from lookalikes, LinkedIn, your CSV or your existing book, then enrich every record.

2

Research

Each contact carries a company summary, personal summary, stated priorities and a conversion angle before anyone reaches out.

3

Reach

Email and LinkedIn, sequenced deliberately. Never three channels inside twelve days, because saturation kills scarce relationships.

4

Signal

Opens, clicks and replies continuously re-rank the list, so the next call is always the one most likely to convert.

5

Convert

Dial from the platform with a script grounded only in known facts, and a live coach reading the call as it happens.

6

Capture

Recording, transcript, scored review and extracted follow-ups on every conversation, written straight to the contact record.

7

Learn

Outcomes update close probability, pipeline and the coaching digest, so the next campaign starts smarter than the last.

Why this is different

Scripts and emails are built only from what is actually known about a contact. The system refuses to invent a prior conversation, a statistic or a mutual connection.
